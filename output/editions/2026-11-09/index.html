Persistent Behavioral Learning: Giving AI Agents Lasting Decision Context

A new framework enables autonomous agents to retain human corrections across sessions instead of repeating mistakes.

Researchers have introduced Forge Decision Context, an architecture designed to solve a fundamental limitation of large language model agents: failing to retain behavioral corrections across sessions. While existing memory systems primarily record factual entities and dialogue history, Decision Context captures behavioral feedback from human corrections and tool-call failures. It compiles these signals into imperative rules and injects them precisely at relevant decision points, reaching 95 percent compliance on deterministic benchmarks while significantly cutting token overhead.

Teaching models procedural rules across sessions bridges the critical gap between clumsy chatbots and reliable digital coworkers.

Asahi Linux Abandons MIT License for GPL Over Uncredited AI Scraping

The Apple Silicon Linux port adopts copyleft terms to safeguard maintainer labor against automated code extraction.

The Asahi Linux project, dedicated to porting the Linux operating system to Apple Silicon hardware, has announced an immediate license transition from permissive MIT terms to the GNU General Public License (GPL). Maintainers enacted the change after discovering unreleased development code scraped and surfaced by commercial AI coding tools without attribution or permission. Adopting GPL enforces strict copyleft requirements, ensuring that downstream projects and automated tools utilizing their reverse-engineered breakthroughs remain publicly accessible and ethically accountable.

Open source projects are actively reconsidering permissive licensing models to prevent commercial AI tools from exploiting unreleased work.
